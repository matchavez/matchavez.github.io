# Christchurch Golf Information

A strategy & yardage hub for golf courses around Christchurch, New Zealand. Tee-club recommendations are calibrated for **calm conditions at 20°C** and recalculated per tee and temperature; adjust for wind using the Reference page. All distances are **on-card metres**.
